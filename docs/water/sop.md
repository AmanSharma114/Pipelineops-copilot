# Probe monitoring operating procedure
Version 1.0

## Safe restart
Retain the sealed manifest and original readings. Ask Instrument Operations for a corrected export covering the same probe and sequence bounds. Rerun the complete export and publish its report only after validation succeeds.

## Verification
Compare the unique sample count with last minus first plus one. For the supplied PROBE-17 sample, sequences 120 through 122 yield three readings with a minimum of 0.38 NTU and a maximum of 0.46 NTU.
